# SE-AI Mind (Darwin 0.1) - Codename System

## Philosophy

Codenames for SE-AI generations must be:
- **Globally recognizable** - Known across cultures and languages
- **Memorable** - Easy to remember after hearing once
- **Pronounceable** - Clear pronunciation in English and major languages
- **Culturally sticky** - Has existing cultural resonance
- **Thematically meaningful** - Connects to the generation's breakthrough
- **Journalist-friendly** - Easy for press to repeat and write about

## Generation 1: Darwin

**Status**: ASSIGNED
**Version**: 0.1.x
**Breakthrough**: Evolution infrastructure itself
**Reason**: Charles Darwin established the theory of evolution by natural selection. The first SE-AI generation establishes the evolution engine - the ability for AI to improve itself through iteration, selection, and inheritance.
**Risk Assessment**:
- Trademark: LOW (historical figure, public domain)
- Cultural: LOW (universally positive association with science)
- Confusion: LOW (not used by major tech products)
- International: LOW (Darwin known worldwide)

## Assignment Process

Before assigning a codename:

1. **Recognizability Test** - Survey 10+ people across demographics
2. **Thematic Fit** - Map to generation's primary breakthrough
3. **Trademark Search** - Check USPTO and international registries
4. **Cultural Association Audit** - Check for negative connotations
5. **Confusion Check** - Search for existing products/services with name
6. **International Meaning** - Verify no offensive meanings in top 20 languages
7. **Search/SEO Analysis** - Check search volume and competition
8. **Stakeholder Review** - Internal team approval
